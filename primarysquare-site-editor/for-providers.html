The problem

The first visit shouldn't be an excavation.

Records scattered across four portals. A history told for the fifth time. Half an appointment spent working out what you're looking at before you can start looking after someone.

When a member shares their Health Story with you, that work is already done. Their records gathered from national networks, their own history, their measurements, organized and current.

Care begins where it would normally arrive after months.

Your panel

Build the panel you want.

Tell members what your practice is genuinely good at: the conditions you treat well, the people you understand, the way you like to work.

They search on fit. Not on who happens to be nearby and in-network.

Panel composition becomes a choice.

The members

An audience already paying attention.

Our members have gathered their records, learned their numbers, and gone looking for a practice that will work with them. They arrive informed, motivated and ready to begin.

A complete picture, up front

Conditions, medications, labs, imaging and care team, assembled before the first appointment rather than during it.

Shared on the member's terms

Members decide what to share and with whom, and can see every access. Trust is built into the handoff.

Engaged from day one

People who track their health, understand their numbers and have chosen you deliberately tend to stay, follow through and refer.

Found for what you do

Your specialisms, your approach and your availability, described in your words, searched by people looking for exactly that.

In practice

Less time on the system. More time on the person.

Designed to take weight off a practice rather than add a system to it.

  • Secure messagingWith members and their care team, in one thread.
  • SchedulingAppointments that already know who is coming and why.
  • Intake and consentCompleted before the visit, from the member's own Health Story.
  • Shared care plansOne plan the member, the family and the practice can all see.

Native EHR integrations and a web-based provider portal are in development, with broader practice tools to follow.
